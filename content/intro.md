This site aims to share education modules to help learn about how to incorporate AI in medicine. 

## How to Navigate

Feel free to click through the tabs on the left panel to navigate through the educational content. For each module, you will find:

- A link to a slideshow presentation
- Speaker notes to correspond with the slideshow
- An embedded YouTube video of slides with voiceover
- A link to a Google Colab notebook to practice
- A collection of links to relevant resources for that module
